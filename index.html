Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the CoreStack ecosystem.

How is CoreStack different from traditional college clubs? ▼

CoreStack is completely independent, builder-run, and focused 100% on execution. We don't host theoretical presentations or textbook sessions—we create competitive 24-hour arenas and high-intensity sprints where software actually gets deployed.

Is participation in CoreStack hackathons free? ▼

Yes, 100% free. We believe true technical talent should never have a financial gate. Shortlisted teams receive free access, 1Gbps WiFi, meals, energy drinks, and mentor access throughout the sprint.

What is the team size requirement for hackathons? ▼

Teams can have 2 to 4 members. If you are a solo builder looking for teammates, we facilitate matchmaking in our community channels before the event kicks off.

How can colleges or student clubs collaborate with CoreStack? ▼

We actively partner with campus coding clubs, technical societies, and student bodies across Hyderabad as community partners. Reach out to us via our contact channels to co-host or cross-collaborate.
